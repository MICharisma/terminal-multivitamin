The Andean potato (Solanum tuberosum) reached the páramo gardens of
northern Ecuador by the late sixteenth century (Murra 1980; Salomon 1986).
Later surveys of Chimborazo cultivars (Zimmerer 1996) and a re-reading of
the visitas (Espinoza Soriano 1976) still frame the debate. Compare also
Horkheimer (1973) on coastal irrigation, and the field notes in
Oberem (1980: 44-52). A dissenting view appears in Larson (1995).
